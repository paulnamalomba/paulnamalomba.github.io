Targeted Preparation Pages

These pages focus narrowly on the public themes around Dr. Obiora Nnene: transport systems, modelling, simulation, optimisation, transport big data, and practical AI/ML in transport and infrastructure. They are built for fast preparation, not broad theory review.

What the research pattern suggests
  • Core methods: agent-based modelling, simulation-based optimisation, meta-heuristics, activity-based demand modelling.
  • Data lens: real operational data, imperfect transport data, GTFS/AFC-style workflows, validation under uncertainty.
  • Decision lens: trade-offs between user cost, operator cost, accessibility, service quality, emissions, and implementation feasibility.
  • Current extensions: EV policy, transport decarbonisation, computer vision, and AI for infrastructure and urban systems.

How To Use These Pages

Start with the mock interview to expose weak spots. Then move to the answer templates and adapt the wording to your own examples from SEAT, SESKA, ComputeMore, and teaching. Finish with the revision sheet immediately before the assessment.

The aim is not to sound scripted. The aim is to be structured, fast, and evidence-backed when he probes modelling choices, data quality, optimisation trade-offs, or practical AI use.
